Our Services

myMedVia handles every aspect of your medical journey so you can focus on recovery.

What's Included

  • Medical Consultation — Connect with a spine surgeon before you travel
  • Diagnostic Imaging — MRI and CT scans at partner facilities
  • Treatment Planning — Personalized surgical plan based on your condition
  • Visa Assistance — Invitation letters for medical travel visas
  • Travel Coordination — Flights, airport transfers, accommodations
  • Interpreter Services — multilingual support throughout your stay
  • Follow-up Care — Post-discharge monitoring and remote consultations

Patient Journey

  1. Initial consultation and medical record review
  2. Treatment plan and cost estimate
  3. Travel arrangements and visa support
  4. Arrival and hospital admission
  5. Diagnostic tests and pre-operative assessment
  6. Surgery
  7. Recovery and discharge planning
  8. Follow-up care coordination
