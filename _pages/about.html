About myMedVia

Breaking down barriers to world-class medical care.

Our Story

myMedVia was founded after our founder witnessed a family member endure 18 months on a Canadian waitlist for spinal surgery—watching chronic pain erode quality of life while waiting for care that ultimately required an emergency surgery that could have been planned.

We asked: Why does geography determine wait time? Why should patients choose between financial ruin and living in pain? Our answer: Connect patients with the world's best surgeons, wherever they are, at transparent, fair prices.

Our Values

Patient-First

Every decision starts with "What's best for the patient?" No commission-based referrals. No steering to specific surgeons. Honest, unbiased recommendations.

Transparency

Clear pricing. Detailed timelines. Real success rates. No hidden fees or vague promises. You know exactly what to expect.

Accessibility

Healthcare should transcend borders. We handle the complexity of medical travel so you can focus on healing.

Quality

We partner only with JCI-accredited hospitals and internationally trained surgeons with proven track records. Quality is non-negotiable.
