Medical Questions

Lumbar disc herniation occurs when the soft center of a spinal disc (nucleus pulposus) pushes through a tear in the tougher outer ring (annulus fibrosus). This can press on nearby nerves, causing pain, numbness, or weakness in the lower back and legs. It's commonly called a slipped or ruptured disc.

Surgery is typically recommended when: (1) Conservative treatments (physical therapy, medications, injections) have failed after 6+ weeks, (2) You have progressive muscle weakness, (3) Pain significantly impacts daily life and sleep, (4) Cauda equina syndrome is present (emergency). Your surgeon will review your MRI and symptoms to determine the best approach.

Both are minimally invasive techniques to remove herniated disc material. Microdiscectomy uses a small incision (1-2 inches) with a microscope. Endoscopic discectomy uses an even smaller incision (less than 1 inch) with a camera (endoscope) and specialized instruments. Endoscopic typically offers faster recovery and less tissue disruption, but may not be suitable for all cases.

Microdiscectomy has a 90-95% success rate for relieving leg pain (radiculopathy). Endoscopic discectomy shows comparable results in appropriate candidates. Spinal fusion success rates vary depending on the indication (degenerative disc disease vs. spondylolisthesis), typically 70-90% for pain relief and fusion success.

With myMedVia, once you submit your medical records and we confirm your treatment plan, you can typically be in surgery within 5-7 days of arriving in China. There's no waitlist—we coordinate all appointments in advance so your trip is efficient.

Travel & Logistics

Most patients stay 7-10 days: arrival day (pre-op tests), Day 2 (MRI + surgeon consultation), Day 3-4 (surgery), Day 5-6 (hospital recovery), Day 7-10 (light activity and discharge clearance). Your coordinator provides a detailed daily schedule.

Most nationalities require a visa. We provide an official invitation letter specifically for medical treatment (M-visa category) that you submit with your visa application. Some countries have 72-hour visa-free transit options. Your coordinator will guide you through the process.

Our standard package includes: surgeon fees, hospital stay, pre-op tests, MRI, medication during hospitalization, airport pickup, accommodation coordination, translator services, and 6-month remote follow-up. International airfare and visa fees are not included.

Costs & Insurance

Main factors: lower administrative and insurance costs, government healthcare subsidies, high surgical volume leading to efficiency, and favorable exchange rates. The hospitals we partner with are JCI-accredited and use the same implants and equipment as Western hospitals—the cost savings come from healthcare system differences, not quality compromises.

Some international insurance plans may reimburse for treatment abroad, though many don't. We can provide all documentation (diagnosis, treatment plan, receipts) needed for your insurance claim. We recommend checking with your insurer before proceeding. We do not guarantee insurance reimbursement.

We provide remote follow-up for 6 months post-surgery. You can message your care coordinator with photos or concerns, and we'll connect you with your surgeon. If there's a local complication, having your complete medical records in English allows any local doctor to continue your care seamlessly.
