Treatment Options

From conservative approaches to minimally invasive surgery, there are multiple proven paths to relief. Your surgeon will recommend the most appropriate treatment based on your specific condition.

Non-Surgical Treatments

Physical Therapy

Targeted exercises to strengthen core muscles, improve flexibility, and relieve pressure on nerves. Typically 6-12 weeks of consistent therapy.

Medications

NSAIDs, muscle relaxants, and nerve pain medications (gabapentin, pregabalin) to manage pain and inflammation during healing.

Epidural Steroid Injections

Corticosteroid injected near compressed nerves to reduce inflammation and provide temporary relief. Can be effective for 3-12 months.

Activity Modification

Avoiding activities that aggravate symptoms, using proper lifting techniques, and ergonomic adjustments to reduce disc stress.

Surgical Treatments

Microdiscectomy

The gold standard for lumbar disc herniation surgery. A small incision (1-2 inches) allows the surgeon to remove herniated disc material pressing on nerves using a microscope for precision.

  • Surgery time: 1-2 hours
  • Hospital stay: 1-2 days
  • Recovery: 2-4 weeks to desk work
  • Success rate: 90-95%

Endoscopic Discectomy

The least invasive surgical option. A tiny incision (less than 1 inch) uses an endoscope with a camera and laser to remove disc material. Fastest recovery profile.

  • Surgery time: 1 hour
  • Hospital stay: Same-day or 1 night
  • Recovery: 1-2 weeks to desk work
  • Success rate: 85-90%

Starting from

$6,000

Spinal Fusion

For severe cases with instability or when previous surgeries haven't worked. The surgeon removes the damaged disc and fuses adjacent vertebrae using bone graft and implants.

  • Surgery time: 3-5 hours
  • Hospital stay: 3-5 days
  • Recovery: 6-12 weeks to light activity
  • Success rate: 70-90%

Starting from

$12,000

Artificial Disc Replacement

Motion-preserving alternative to fusion. The damaged disc is removed and replaced with a metal and plastic implant that maintains spine mobility.

  • Surgery time: 2-3 hours
  • Hospital stay: 2-3 days
  • Recovery: 4-8 weeks to light activity
  • Success rate: 80-90%

Starting from

$15,000
